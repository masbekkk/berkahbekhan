# Video direction: TikTok + Reels for client acquisition

## Product campaign profiles

When `brand/<projectSlug>/product-profile.json` exists, read it together with the project's truth inventory before applying the generic service-sales guidance below. Its audience, offer and CTA take precedence for that product campaign.

For BerkahEmas, use `brand/berkahemas/product-profile.json` and `projects/berkahemas/project-truth.json`. Sell ready-to-use **Software Toko Emas** with **Minta Demo BerkahEmas**. BerkahBekhan remains the producer. The three prepared storyboards are in `content/tiktok/berkahemas/`; no finished videos or recorder extensions are included.

This direction extends the existing `v2-design` feed direction into short-form video.

## Goal

Turn real software work into short videos that stop relevant viewers, prove capability, create desire for a similar system, and generate Indonesian UMKM/service-business inquiries.

The conversion path is:

`scroll -> attention -> relevance -> proof -> desire -> profile/DM -> qualified project conversation`

Reach/FYP is useful only when it moves the right viewers further down that path.

## Core rules

**AI directs the demo. The real application performs the demo.**

**Do not record the app in menu order. Record it in persuasion order.**

AI may inspect the project, identify verified value, plan a result-first story, choose demo data, direct camera/zoom/cut decisions, write concise Indonesian overlays/voiceover, and compose the edit. Every visible product state must still come from the real local/staging application or real simulator/device.

## What “sales video” means here

Sales is part of the recording/story itself—not a CTA pasted onto a technical walkthrough.

A strong demo should make the viewer experience:

1. **Attention** — an immediately interesting real product state.
2. **Relevance** — a task/problem/desire the target business recognizes.
3. **Proof** — the real application performs the workflow.
4. **Payoff** — the useful result becomes obvious.
5. **Action** — a low-friction invitation to ask for a similar build.

## Default 15-25 second structure

- **0-2s — Result-first hook:** strongest truthful state/action. No greeting/logo intro.
- **2-5s — Relevance:** concise buyer problem/task/desire.
- **5-15s — Proof:** 2-4 fast real interactions with visible cause/effect.
- **12-20s — Payoff:** show the completed state and business capability.
- **last 2-4s — CTA:** invite a relevant inquiry without invented scarcity.

The phases may overlap. Do not stretch a weak concept to hit a duration.

## Content priorities

1. **Problem -> real solution** — show a common business task and a working implementation.
2. **Result-first product demo** — tease the strongest outcome, then prove how it happens.
3. **Feature proof** — one capability with observable cause/effect.
4. **Before -> after workflow** — only when both states are truthful and the “before” is not falsely attributed to a client.
5. **Behind the work** — implementation/process when it increases buyer trust.
6. **Service invitation** — a direct invitation to discuss a similar need.

## Hook direction

Prefer buyer-facing hooks:

- `Customer bisa booking sendiri dari website.`
- `Order baru langsung masuk ke dashboard.`
- `Website ini bukan cuma company profile.`
- `Kalau booking masih lewat chat satu-satu...`

Avoid developer-first hooks in client-acquisition videos:

- `Built with Next.js 16`
- `My new React project`
- `Today I will show...`

Tech can appear later as credibility, not as the main reason a business owner should care.

## Demonstration direction

Prefer interactions with visible change:

`input -> submit -> result`
`filter -> dashboard changes`
`customer action -> admin state updates`
`selection -> calculated/confirmed result`

Skip or speed through dead navigation, authentication setup, loading, repeated scrolling, and anything that does not support the sales story.

It is allowed to show the final result first, then replay the real steps that create it.

## Demo data

Prepare safe demo data intentionally so the real interface is easy to understand on a phone screen. Use realistic but fictional names, products/services, statuses, dates, and amounts when appropriate. Never expose real customer data or fabricate testimonials/results.

## Sales language

Prefer capability/value statements:

- `Pelanggan bisa pilih jadwal langsung dari website.`
- `Order masuk dan bisa dicek dari satu dashboard.`
- `Admin bisa filter laporan berdasarkan periode.`
- `Butuh website/app dengan alur seperti ini? Kirim jenis usaha dan kebutuhanmu lewat DM.`

Avoid:

- invented ROI/conversion percentages
- unverified claims like `meningkatkan penjualan`
- fake client pain points presented as facts
- fake deadlines, discounts, or scarcity
- generic hype that the footage cannot prove

When the project is not commissioned work, label it accurately as a project/build/concept implementation.

## Visual + pacing direction

The software remains the hero.

Use:

- real screen recording
- readable 9:16 composition
- visual change every ~1-3 seconds where natural
- purposeful punch-in/zoom on the active control or result
- short Indonesian overlays
- captions/voiceover that explain value rather than narrating clicks
- a clear payoff frame before CTA

Avoid fake device mockups, generated dashboards, generic AI b-roll, excessive motion graphics, and decorative scenes that delay the product proof.

## CTA strategy

The CTA should continue the story, not feel pasted on.

If the video demonstrates booking:
`Bisnis kamu butuh alur booking seperti ini? DM jenis usaha + kebutuhannya.`

If it demonstrates ordering:
`Butuh sistem order yang sesuai alur bisnismu? Kita bisa bahas lewat DM.`

If it demonstrates a dashboard:
`Kalau operasionalmu butuh dashboard seperti ini, kirim kebutuhannya lewat DM.`

## Success metrics

Evaluate two layers:

**Attention/retention:** first-seconds hold, completion, rewatches, shares/saves, profile visits.

**Business:** DMs, qualified inquiries, discovery conversations, proposals, and projects won.

A 10k-view demo that creates qualified project inquiries is more successful than a 500k-view meme that attracts no buyers.
