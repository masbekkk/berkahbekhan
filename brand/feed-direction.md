# Feed direction: websites for UMKM

This direction implements the October 2026 feed refactor. It takes precedence over older examples that require technical headlines, a screenshot on every slide, or a nine-slide narrative.

## Audience and message

Speak to Indonesian UMKM and service businesses considering a website. Lead with what a person can do with the work. Use concise Indonesian headlines; put implementation details in captions. Keep each project's real industry and scope visible. A school system is evidence of capability, not an invented UMKM commission.

Portfolio remains the account's foundation. A starting nine-post mix is five examples of work, two client questions, one actual process story, and one service invitation. This is an editorial experiment, not a quota or engagement guarantee. A single project may support several distinct stories, but repeated crops alone do not create new stories.

## Four reusable compositions

| Master | Primary material | Composition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Selected Work | Real product interface | Short headline above one large, intentional UI crop; project and sector credit. |
| Useful Feature | One verified interaction | Focused control or workflow crop; headline and short explanation; light or dark canvas. |
| Behind the Work | Real iteration, source excerpt, or recorded process | A specific decision and its evidence; never a fabricated process photo. |
| Service Invitation | Clear offer and inquiry action | Typography may lead; one small, labeled work sample can provide context. |

These are publishing roles layered on the seven composition families in `visual-rules.md`, not four identical templates. Selected Work maps to Project Hero/Product Screenshot; Useful Feature to Feature Spotlight/Project Detail; Behind the Work to Development Insight/Technical Breakdown. Service Invitation is an additional commercial composition.

## Visual decisions

- Feed frames are 1080 x 1350 with 80px safe margins. Use the existing 8px spacing scale and brand typography.
- Use Inter for client-facing copy and JetBrains Mono only for quiet metadata and actual code. Keep cover headlines short, normally two lines.
- Use neutral light and dark canvases from `colors.md`. Let authentic screenshots carry project colors. Accent colors are optional, not mandatory badges.
- Preserve screenshot geometry through uniform scaling and clipping. Crop to the controls that explain the claim; do not export unrelated personal records from surrounding tables.
- Product posts prioritize readable UI. Service invitations and client questions may prioritize type. Do not force a 60-85% screenshot quota onto every post.
- Use real Auto Layout, editable text, separate image layers, bound variables, and shared text styles. Change image emphasis and content order between compositions.
- Remove ornamental grids, fake browser chrome, nested cards, repetitive category pills, and stacks displayed as sales benefits.
- Keep every post understandable alone. A feed overview is a planning aid, not a puzzle spread across nine posts.

## Copy and proof

Use one factual point per post. Prefer "Pilih kelas dan semester. Unduh satu PDF." to "memory-efficient document synthesis". A service CTA can say "Kirim jenis usaha dan kebutuhanmu lewat DM." Do not invent prices, turnaround promises, testimonials, measured improvements, or delivery history.

Distinguish available functionality from executed validation. Source code proves an implementation exists; it does not establish current production availability, user counts, or conversion results. Label excerpts and conceptual workflow diagrams accurately.

## Review

Inspect full-size exports, a roughly 360px-wide preview, and cover thumbnails. Check the intended headline and focal controls, rather than requiring every peripheral screenshot label to be readable. Verify every claim, image crop, text boundary, and CTA. Planned grid slots must be clearly labeled and excluded from production exports.
